Developer tools

I've developed artist-facing tools and pipeline improvements across Houdini, Unreal, Unity, Maya, Blender, and in-house engines. My work ranges from small scripts for repetitive tasks to tools for debugging, asset validation, content generation, and day-to-day workflow support. Coming from an art background, I’m interested in making tools useful and understandable to the people working with them. I enjoy finding out what slows artists down and building practical solutions that fit how they work—whether that means a new tool, automating a tedious task, or simplifying an existing workflow.

Some examples: - Debugging tools: Helpers for investigating issues in Pax Dei, alongside collision-debugging tools for Unreal. - Asset validation and processing: An Unreal asset validator, an HLOD generator for Pax Dei - Various batch-processing utilities to optimize performance and catch problematic assets. - Version-control helpers: Perforce tools for Houdini and Maya. - Script distribution: A tool for distributing scripts to Maya users. - Artist launcher: A launcher to bring common artist tasks and tools into one place.
